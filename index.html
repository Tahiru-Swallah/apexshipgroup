Frequently Asked Questions

Common Logistics Queries

How does ApexShip Group provide live satellite tracking?
Every package dispatched through ApexShip is linked to a GPS/cellular telemetry barcode. Scan updates automatically refresh on your dashboard at every sorting hub, port exit, and driver vehicle transfer.
What insurance coverage options are included with shipments?
Standard shipments include basic declared value protection up to $100. Our premium Cargo Loss Protection add-on guarantees 100% full invoice replacement for lost or damaged goods during transit.
How are international customs duties and tariffs handled?
ApexShip offers both DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) and DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid) services. Our digital customs clearance engine pre-files duty paperwork to prevent border holds.
